refactor(cinbase): one record list declares every cinbase setting

No separate issue; the record list is the whole story.

`python/cinbase/config.py` answered each setting through several hand-written
lists that drifted apart: the constructor defaults, the settings-page rows,
the save-loop fallbacks and the table-reload key sequence each restated the
same facts. One list now declares every setting once and everything derives
from it.

- `CONFIG_RECORDS` is the single declaration of each setting:
  (key, default, control, table_affecting, mirror, effect, overrides);
  `CinBaseConfig.__init__` seeds its attributes from the list.
- The shipped input-method config.json files are regenerated by the
  deterministic `write_shipped_configs()` writer.
- The settings page script derives its defaults, the sel-keys option rows
  and the save-loop fallbacks from the records and selKeys fields now served
  on /config; `checkConfigChange` iterates the table-affecting rows instead
  of a hand-written key sequence.
- keyboardLayout, colorCandWnd and directCommitString (JSON-only orphans)
  are the end-to-end demo of one row propagating.

Validation:

- `python/python3/python.exe` `-m unittest discover -s tests -p "test_cinbase_config_keys.py"`
  on the integration tree carrying this diff: `Ran 12 tests ... OK` — every
  record projects its default, control and effect onto the engine-read
  values, and the table-affecting rows drive the reload.
