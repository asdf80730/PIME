## Problem

Twenty-five scripts re-type the same git-root prologue, nineteen their own
`mktemp -d` sandbox, nine the `trap cleanup EXIT`, six the `cygpath -w`
temp-dir dance, and five the same `g++ -std=c++17 -O1 -I PIMETextService`
recipe. A fix to one is a fix to none, and a misquoted expansion turns a gate
into a silent no-op.

## Solution

One sourced `tests/gate_lib.sh` exposing `gate_root`, `gate_sandbox`,
`gate_run` and `cpp_test <src> [libs] [wine]`; every check and self-test keeps
only its assertions. The orphan sibling gets its home as a filter case of the
gate runner.
